# Browser Loot privacy policy

Browser Loot processes visual details locally to create your collection. It does not operate a backend, create accounts, upload collections, show advertisements, or send analytics or crash reports.

## What stays in your browser

Saved specimens include visual style values, the source website origin, collection date, favourite state, and local preview assets where available. The Word Familiar stores your chosen word, a total feeding count, and each website's most recent feeding date. Surrounding page text is not saved. Your settings and exclusions stay in extension-local storage. Specimens and images use extension IndexedDB.

Screenshots are taken only when requested. They may contain private information visible on your screen. You review and optionally centre-crop each image before saving. A page URL is included only when you select that option. Unconfirmed screenshots are held in popup memory and discarded when cancelled or closed.

## Network requests

During collection, Browser Loot may request the favicon or cursor image from a website origin you have authorised. Those requests omit cookies and referrer headers. No third-party favicon provider is used. The saved museum does not contact source websites to render previews. Following a source link opens that website normally.

## Permissions

`activeTab` permits deliberate collection and screenshot capture from the invoked tab. `scripting` runs the collector. `storage` saves settings. Optional HTTP/HTTPS host access allows automatic collection and fetching source assets. Browser controls can revoke access; Settings can pause collection, disable individual types, or exclude websites. Private browsing is disabled.

## Control and retention

Finds remain until deleted or the extension is removed, subject to browser storage behaviour. The application stops accepting new captures at 250 MB. You can export a ZIP backup, delete individual finds, or clear the entire collection and familiar counts in Settings. Backups contain your source websites and saved screenshots; manage them as private files. Exported PNG sheets omit source labels.

Firefox's data declaration states that no data is transmitted for external collection. Source-asset requests are limited to the locally requested collection function. Any future change to transmission behaviour requires a corresponding privacy and consent update.
